Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

The short version: Squishy Dumpling does not collect, store or share any personal information about anyone, including children.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party software development kits (SDKs). We do not collect names, emails, photos, locations, device identifiers or usage data.

What stays on the device

Game progress (levels, stars, collected cards, chosen dumpling pack and boosters) is saved only on the device, so the game remembers where you got to. It never leaves the device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see payment details. Apple tells the app only which items were bought, so they can be unlocked and restored. Purchases are behind a grown-up check in the app.

Game Center

If Game Center is turned on for the device, the game may report achievements to Apple's Game Center. Game Center is provided and controlled by Apple, and parents can manage or turn it off in Screen Time. We do not receive any personal information from Game Center. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Squishy Dumpling is made for children and is listed in the App Store's Kids Category. Because we collect no personal information, we have nothing to disclose, sell or share. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's Kids Category rules. There are no external links in the game for children to follow, and no chat or messaging.

Data retention and security

We retain no personal data, so there is nothing to retain, delete or secure on our side. On-device progress is protected by the device's own security and removed when the app is deleted.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before any change takes effect.
