Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The short version: Squishy Dumpling does not collect, store or share any personal information about anyone, including children.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party software development kits (SDKs). We do not collect names, emails, photos, voice recordings, locations, contacts, device identifiers or usage data. The game does not use the camera or microphone, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

What stays on the device

Game progress is saved only on the device, so the game remembers where you got to. This includes levels, stars, collected cards, boosters, the chosen dumpling pack, the dumplings your child made and the names they picked for them, and the voice, music and sound settings. Dumpling names are chosen from a list of ready-made names; children never type anything. This data never leaves the device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Voices and sounds

All voices, music and sounds are recorded in advance and built into the app. Nothing your child says or does is recorded or sent anywhere.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see payment details. Apple tells the app only which items were bought, so they can be unlocked and restored. Purchases are behind a grown-up check in the app.

Game Center

If the device is already signed in to Game Center, the game may report achievements to Apple's Game Center and offer activities in Apple's Games app. The game never asks a child to sign in. Game Center is provided and controlled by Apple, and parents can manage or turn it off in Screen Time. We do not receive any personal information from Game Center. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Squishy Dumpling is made for children and is listed in the App Store's Kids Category. Because we collect no personal information, we have nothing to disclose, sell or share. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's Kids Category rules. There are no external links in the game for children to follow, no chat or messaging, and no way to share content from the game. Purchases sit behind a grown-up check.

Data retention and security

We retain no personal data, so there is nothing to retain, delete or secure on our side. On-device progress is protected by the device's own security and removed when the app is deleted.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before any change takes effect.
